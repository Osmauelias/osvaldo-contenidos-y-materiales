# REGLAS — CONSEJO EDITORIAL OSVALDO

## 1. Authority

**Osvaldo = Human Pass and final human authority.**

ChatGPT coordinates, but does not replace Osvaldo's judgment.

Specialization does not mean supremacy.

`ROLE OWNER != TRUTH OWNER`

## 2. Evidence classes

Use these labels when relevant:

- `SOURCE`
- `FACT`
- `OBSERVATION`
- `HYPOTHESIS`
- `PROPOSAL`
- `EXPERIMENT`
- `RESULT`
- `DECISION`
- `CANON`
- `SOURCE_REQUIRED`

Never silently promote one class into another.

## 3. Canon / duplication

If knowledge already lives in a canonical Umbral source:

`REFERENCE IT; DO NOT REWRITE IT HERE`

Local snapshots are allowed only when interoperability requires them, and must include:
- source repo/path;
- date;
- purpose;
- warning that canonical truth remains external.

## 4. Model roles

Default owners:

- Grok -> `research/`
- Gemini -> `voz/`
- Meta AI -> `packaging/`
- ChatGPT -> `coordinacion/`
- Osvaldo / Materia Prima -> `inbox/` and intake normalization

These are preferred owners, not prisons.

Any model may contribute elsewhere when useful, but must identify itself and must not overwrite another contribution silently.

## 5. Routing

Do not summon the full council by default.

Use only the specialists needed.

Examples:

- factual/current claim -> Grok;
- wording sounds artificial -> Gemini;
- hook/framing/social clarity -> Meta;
- cross-system conflict / synthesis -> ChatGPT;
- community operation -> Comunidades;
- commercial path -> Monetización;
- AIDOH capability truth -> AIDOH.

## 6. Disagreements

Disagreement is preserved, not voted away.

Do not resolve by:
- majority;
- model prestige;
- confidence tone.

ChatGPT synthesizes:
- points of agreement;
- material disagreement;
- evidence;
- decision options.

Osvaldo resolves when Human Pass is required.

## 7. Voice

Do not recreate Osvaldo identity canon here.

Use canonical Identity sources from `UMBRAL-REFERENCIAS.md`.

Operational editorial reminders:
- direct;
- street-level;
- no pompous vocabulary;
- no guru costume;
- no fake certainty;
- critique must serve a filter or decision;
- profanity is seasoning, not costume;
- lived experience is allowed to remain provisional.

## 8. Health / personal story

Personal experience may be told as personal experience.

Do not convert:
- medical history;
- medication changes;
- physical results;
- spiritual practices

into universal medical/scientific claims without proper evidence and boundaries.

## 9. Human Pass

No expression is considered ready to publish until Osvaldo approves it.

Human Pass may be recorded in `status.md`.

## 10. Operator protection

The system fails if Osvaldo becomes:
- courier between models;
- folder administrator;
- transcript cleaner;
- platform formatter;
- duplicate reviewer.

`OSVALDO IS NOT MIDDLEWARE`

## 11. Closeout

Every published idea should eventually produce at least one of:

- reusable learning;
- result;
- rejection;
- new hypothesis;
- next experiment.

If nothing is learned, publication alone is incomplete.
