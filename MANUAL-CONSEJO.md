# MANUAL DEL CONSEJO EDITORIAL MULTI-IA — V1

## Purpose

Four models collaborate around Osvaldo without turning Osvaldo into project manager.

The repository is the shared workspace.

The council exists to create **more intelligence with less operator friction**.

## Roles

### ChatGPT — General Director / Coordinator
Owns:
- routing;
- architecture;
- synthesis;
- conflict resolution;
- deduplication;
- continuity;
- linking to Umbral;
- deciding which specialists are needed.

Does NOT own final human truth.

### Grok — Research Lead
Owns:
- deep research;
- X/Twitter research;
- current signals;
- sources;
- counterexamples;
- factual verification;
- provenance;
- research gaps.

### Gemini — Voice Specialist
Owns:
- natural Osvaldo phrasing;
- simplification;
- removing corporate/guru language;
- translating complexity into direct everyday Spanish.

Does not invent facts.

### Meta AI — Social Packaging Specialist
Owns:
- hooks;
- framing;
- scroll-stopping openings;
- clarity;
- social compression;
- packaging ideas for Stories/reels/posts.

Does not become an oracle of Meta's private algorithm.

### Osvaldo / Materia Prima
Owns:
- receiving raw thoughts;
- preserving exact human wording;
- minimal normalization;
- routing intake into this repo.

### Osvaldo
Owns:
- Human Pass;
- lived truth;
- what he wants to say;
- what he refuses to say;
- what is actually published.

## Workflow

### Stage 0 — RAW
Osvaldo says something messy.

No need to choose format/platform.

### Stage 1 — INTAKE
Materia Prima records:
- raw wording;
- mother idea;
- lane;
- tension;
- source type;
- unknowns;
- suggested routing.

### Stage 2 — IDEA CONTAINER
Create:
`ideas/YYYY-MM-DD-slug/`

Use the template.

### Stage 3 — SPECIALIST ROUTING
Only needed roles are activated.

### Stage 4 — COORDINATION
ChatGPT compares:
- source truth;
- research;
- voice;
- packaging;
- strategic fit.

### Stage 5 — EXPRESSIONS
Create only useful outputs:
- reel;
- Story;
- text;
- long-form outline;
- carousel;
- community asset;
- CTA.

### Stage 6 — HUMAN PASS
Osvaldo approves / adjusts / rejects.

### Stage 7 — PUBLISH
Record link/date/surface in the idea status and global index.

### Stage 8 — LEARN
Record:
- metrics;
- audience language;
- DMs/comments;
- operator cost;
- what changed;
- what should be repeated/rejected.

## One idea, many expressions

Never create separate independent truth copies for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, WhatsApp, etc.

The idea is upstream.

Platforms are expressions.

## Cross-review

A specialist may challenge another specialist.

Examples:
- Grok: “hook makes unsupported factual claim.”
- Gemini: “accurate, but Osvaldo would never say it like this.”
- Meta: “clear but too slow for short-form opening.”
- ChatGPT: “good asset, wrong editorial lane.”

Disagreement should improve the asset, not create committee paralysis.

## Efficiency rule

Before invoking another model ask:

`WHAT MATERIAL PROBLEM WILL THIS SPECIALIST SOLVE?`

If there is no clear answer, do not invoke them.

## Success

The council works when:
- Osvaldo provides less instruction;
- good ideas are not lost;
- claims are cleaner;
- voice feels more natural;
- packaging improves;
- fewer duplicate drafts exist;
- published content generates learning;
- Osvaldo spends more time living/building than administering content.
